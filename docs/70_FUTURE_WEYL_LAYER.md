# Future spectral layer

A logarithmic generator, endpoint/bulk Schur complement, gamma field, or Weyl family is not part of the Green Frame theorem proved here. The frame and Poisson results supply infrastructure for such a layer, but do not by themselves identify a densely defined symmetric operator or a maximal boundary triple.

Any future spectral package must preserve the parameter firewall between the real angular orbit and a complex resolvent parameter, prove its domain statements independently, and keep numerical evidence outside the proof dependency graph.
